Sobre la Autora · About

A letter from the desk of the queen

Unas palabras de mi puño y letra.

Querido lector,

These stories have lived in me for more than ten years. I carried them quietly — a cast of characters, a handful of first kisses, a few endings I already knew by heart — long before I ever let myself write a single word of them down.

Life had other plans first. There were curveballs and hard seasons, years spent in the tech world building things that mattered in a different way. But through all of it, these characters never left. They waited in my heart, patient and stubborn, until I finally found the courage to give them a page.

Choosing to write romance — to trade the certainty of one life for the vulnerability of this one — was the bravest thing I've done. And my hope is simple: that when you read these stories, you feel the passion I poured into them, and that somewhere in them you recognize a little piece of your own heart.

Thank you for being here at the very beginning.

Con cariño,

Reina

En breve · In Short

Reina Acevedo is a New York City–based romance author who blends her background in the tech industry with emotionally charged love stories full of yearning, unforgettable chemistry, and high-stakes romance. Inspired by the idea that even the most logical minds can be undone by the heart, she crafts characters who must navigate ambition, vulnerability, and the risks of falling in love. When she's not writing her next swoon-worthy romance, you'll find her singing, writing song lyrics, and dreaming up stories where passion always has the final word.
