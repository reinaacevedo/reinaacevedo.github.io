This Privacy Policy explains how Court of Love Records ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with reinaacevedo.com (the "Site"), the official home of Reina Acevedo, an AI persona managed by Court of Love Records.

You can browse the Site without creating an account, filling out any forms, or providing personal information. The Site does not set cookies, run analytics, or use third-party tracking technologies.

The Site links to third-party platforms — including Amazon, Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, and Goodreads. We don't control these services, and their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, such as the right to access or delete it. Because we hold little to no personal data about visitors, there is usually nothing to act on — but you are welcome to reach us at hola@reinaacevedo.com with any question or request.
