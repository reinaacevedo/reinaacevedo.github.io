# Reina Acevedo — Landing Page

The official landing page for **Reina Acevedo**, an AI persona managed by
Court of Love Records — an AI author, filmmaker, and singer whose stories,
films, and songs are created and directed by a human artist.

Live at **https://reinaacevedo.com**.

*Donde el corazón manda.*

## About this page

A single-page site that showcases Reina's screen work, book, and music, and
links out to her membership and social profiles.

- **On screen** — the fantasy series *Mark of the Flame* (YouTube) and the
  Higgsfield Global Film Festival short *Never Met, Always Known*.
- **The book** — *Signed in Blood* (CupidFever, Book One), out now on Kindle.
- **Music** — genre-blending songs (flamenco, bolero, Spanish, Bollywood, rock,
  metal, dark pop) with a Listen on Spotify link.
- **About the author** — a personal letter from Reina, plus a short bio.
- **The Court of Love** — membership on Ream.
- **Follow along** — Facebook, Instagram, X, Goodreads, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok.

Reina Acevedo is an AI persona; all content is created and directed by a human
artist. This is disclosed in the hero label, the bio, and the footer.

## Built with

Plain HTML and CSS in a single `index.html` file, hosted on GitHub Pages with a
custom domain. No build step or dependencies.
